● When your remaining liquidity is lower than 10%, Moomoo SG will notify you by email and app notification. Please pay attention to the market conditions and your asset condition, and make sure your remaining liquidity is not lower than 0; that is to say, make sure your equity is not below the maintenance margin.
● When your equity falls below the maintenance margin, Moomoo SG will notify you by email and app notification. You can avoid forced liquidation by depositing funds or closing some of your open positions so that your account equity will remain above the maintenance margin requirement. Please note that Moomoo SG reserves the right to force liquidation at any time when the market fluctuates sharply.
